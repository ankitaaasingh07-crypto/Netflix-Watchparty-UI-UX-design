# Netflix Watch Party UI/UX Design

A UI/UX case study exploring a social viewing experience for Netflix, designed for IIT Indore's Design Odyssey.

Secured **2nd position** in Design Odyssey, an event with a **₹20,000 prize pool**.

## Overview

This project focuses on designing a seamless watch-party experience that allows users to enjoy Netflix content together while being physically apart. The design explores how synchronized viewing, communication, and social interaction can be brought into a familiar streaming interface.

## Problem Statement

Traditional streaming experiences are primarily designed for individual viewing. Friends and groups who want to watch content together remotely often have to rely on separate tools or fragmented experiences.

The goal of this project was to explore a more integrated watch-party experience within a familiar Netflix-style interface.

## Objective

- Explore a social and collaborative streaming experience.
- Keep the interface familiar and easy to navigate.
- Make group viewing interactions feel natural rather than distracting.
- Balance entertainment, communication, and usability.

## Design Process

The project was approached as a UI/UX design exercise, moving from identifying the user experience gap to exploring interface structure, interactions, and visual presentation.

### Key areas explored

- User experience and interaction flow
- Watch-party entry and participation
- Shared viewing experience
- Social interaction during playback
- Information hierarchy and navigation
- Visual consistency with a streaming platform

## Key Features

- Watch-party focused viewing experience
- Social interaction integrated into the viewing flow
- Familiar streaming-platform navigation patterns
- Clear content and interaction hierarchy
- Interface designed with group viewing in mind
